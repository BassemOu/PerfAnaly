How AI Uses Teaching Data

The AI weighs the 3-cycle trend more heavily than any single score. A faculty member rising from 3.2 to 3.8 over three years is rated more favourably than one with a static 4.0. Bonus signals: active learning techniques, high-enrolment management, graduate thesis supervision quality.

How AI Uses Research Data

The AI normalises output by rank and career stage. A 3rd-year Assistant Professor with 1 Q1 publication and 1 grant scores higher than a 10-year Associate Professor with the same output. A zero-grant streak exceeding 3 consecutive years is automatically penalised regardless of publication volume.

How AI Uses Service Data

Service is capped at a 20-point contribution to the composite score. The AI flags over-service (service = 90, research < 60) as an imbalance risk - it may indicate the faculty member is under-investing in research. The pattern is explicitly noted in the AI appraisal summary.

Slide 6 of 8 � Process Flow

Six-Stage Appraisal Workflow

1

Faculty - Self-Assessment Submission

Faculty completes annual self-assessment: uploads publications, grants, teaching materials, and service log. System auto-populates student evaluation scores from the LMS integration.

2

Department Chair - First Review & Scoring

Chair reviews the submission, adds peer observation score, assigns preliminary dimension ratings, and forwards to the Dean with written comments.

3

Division Dean - Divisional Endorsement

Dean reviews Chair scores against divisional benchmarks, can adjust ratings with justification, and endorses or returns the appraisal for re-evaluation.

4

Review Committee - Independent Validation

Cross-disciplinary committee validates scoring consistency, checks for bias, and locks the official composite score used as input for the AI analysis.

5

Provost - AI-Assisted Appraisal Decision

Provost reviews the AI performance analysis, confidence scores, strengths and concerns, and records the official appraisal outcome in the system.

6

HR - Outcome Processing & Notification

HR receives the Provost's appraisal decision, prepares any required documentation, notifies the faculty member, and archives the full appraisal record.

Critical Escalation Triggers

Any single criterion instantly elevates to CRITICAL: student evaluation < 2.5/5.0, zero publications in 3 consecutive years, confirmed academic misconduct, or two consecutive failed PIPs.

Risk Downgrade Path

A faculty member can reduce their risk level by 1 tier per evaluation cycle by meeting all PIP targets and demonstrating measurable upward trend across all three dimensions.

Slide 8 of 8 � Features & User Value

How LuminAI Serves the Provost's Office

Primary End User - Provost's Office

The Provost is the decision authority: LuminAI consolidates scored appraisals from all stages and delivers AI-powered insights so the Provost can make evidence-based, institution-wide faculty decisions.

Institution-Wide Appraisal Dashboard

The Provost sees every faculty member's composite score, risk level, and multi-cycle trend across all departments in a single consolidated view - no manual report collation required.

AI Performance Analysis

GPT-4o generates a ranked insight report per faculty member covering strengths, concerns, trajectory and confidence scoring - giving the Provost objective, evidence-backed input before recording a decision.

Risk Prioritisation Queue

HIGH and CRITICAL faculty are automatically surfaced at the top of the Provost's queue. Each flag includes the specific trigger condition so interventions are targeted and timely.

Formal Decision Recording

The Provost records the official appraisal outcome with written rationale directly in the system. The decision is immediately routed to HR and appended to the permanent, auditable faculty record.

Cross-Cycle Trend Tracking

LuminAI plots score trajectories over multiple appraisal cycles. The Provost can identify sustained underperformance patterns or faculty showing consistent improvement before making a final determination.

Full Audit and Compliance Trail

Every action from faculty submission through Chair, Dean, Committee and Provost is timestamped and actor-attributed. The complete record is exportable for accreditation and governance reporting.

LuminAI gives the Provost's Office a single, authoritative platform to review, decide and archive faculty appraisals across the entire institution.
